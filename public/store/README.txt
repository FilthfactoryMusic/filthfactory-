Full description:
Filthfactory is a UK platform for underground DJs — garage, jungle, bassline, techno and the rest of the floor.

Listening is free. Resident membership is £5 a calendar month (VAT included) to go live from the booth, drop mixes and send gifts. Featured is £15 and advertises your live on Discover.

Live gifts are digital. The DJ receives 50%. Filthfactory receives 50%. DJs are not employees. Gift income is theirs to declare.

You must be 18 or over. You may only broadcast content you have the rights to. Filthfactory does not hold a blanket PRS or PPL licence.
